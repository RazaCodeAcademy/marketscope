Browser extension · In development

Research marketplace products with clarity.

MarketScope is a product research browser extension being developed to help online sellers review publicly available product and listing information, compare pricing, and organize research insights.

The Etsy integration is planned to use the official Etsy Open API, subject to approval and applicable API terms. The extension is not currently represented as approved by or affiliated with Etsy.

Planned features

  • Review permitted public listing information and product attributes.
  • Compare prices and organize products for research.
  • Provide a simple research dashboard for registered users.

Features and data availability depend on the marketplace's API permissions and policies.

Who is it for?

Independent online sellers and product researchers. MarketScope is currently in development and is not accepting public registrations.
